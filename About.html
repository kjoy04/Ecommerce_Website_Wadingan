OUR STORY

A Little Sip of Happiness

BoBa&Brew is a milk tea brand created for people who enjoy refreshing drinks, delicious flavors, and sharing good moments with friends and family.

We believe that even a simple drink can make an ordinary day feel special. That is why our menu features milk tea favorites such as Strawberry, Matcha, Cookies & Cream, and Choco.

Our goal is to make every customer feel welcome and enjoy a delightful milk tea experience with every visit.
